How much does a website cost in India in 2026?

By Agam Sahu, founder of Kodivo · Updated 28 October 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer: a simple business website can cost anywhere from a few thousand rupees to ₹50,000, an online store usually starts around ₹50,000, and custom software can go into lakhs. Here is what decides the price, what you pay every year, and how to avoid surprises.

Typical website prices in India

These are typical market ranges. Prices vary a lot between freelancers, agencies and cities, so use them as a guide, not a fixed rule.

Type of websiteTypical priceGood for
1-page website₹3,000 – ₹15,000Doctors, tutors, shops, freelancers
Business website (5–10 pages)₹15,000 – ₹60,000Clinics, coaching institutes, restaurants, service businesses
Online store (e-commerce)₹50,000 – ₹3,00,000+Brands and shops selling products online
Custom web app or portal₹1,50,000 – ₹20,00,000+CRM, booking platforms, marketplaces, internal tools

What decides the price?

  • Number of pages. More pages means more design, writing and testing.
  • Features. Booking, online payments, login, admin panels and AI chatbots each add work.
  • Design. A ready template is cheaper than a design made only for you.
  • Content. If you give text and photos, it costs less than if they must be written or shot for you.
  • Who builds it. Freelancers usually cost less than agencies. Experience matters more than the label.

Yearly costs after launch

The build price is a one-time cost. These are the running costs to plan for:

ItemTypical yearly cost
Domain name (.com or .in)₹800 – ₹1,200
HostingFree to ₹5,000 for simple sites; more for stores and apps
Business email (optional)About ₹1,500 – ₹2,500 per user
Maintenance (optional)₹999 – ₹3,000 a month, or pay per change
Payment gateway (stores)Usually around 2% of each online payment

How to avoid hidden charges

  • Ask for a written quote with the exact pages, features and number of changes included.
  • Make sure the domain and hosting are bought in your name, not the developer's.
  • Ask who owns the source code and whether you can move to another developer later.
  • Pay in milestones, not 100% upfront.
  • Ask what the yearly renewal will cost, not just the first-year price.

FAQ

Quick answers

What is the cheapest way to get a business website?

A 1-page website with your services, photos, timings, map and a WhatsApp button is the cheapest useful option. Many small businesses start here and add pages later.

Is a free website builder enough?

For a hobby or a very small business, it can be. For a growing business, a proper website is usually faster, ranks better on Google and looks more professional.

How long does it take to make a website?

A simple business website usually takes 3 to 7 days once the content is ready. Online stores take 3 to 6 weeks, and custom apps take longer.
