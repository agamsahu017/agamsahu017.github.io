FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does custom software cost?

Most custom business software we build costs between ₹75,000 and ₹10,00,000, depending on the number of features, users and connections to other systems. You get a fixed quote after a free consultation.

How long does it take?

A focused first version usually takes 4 to 12 weeks. We often launch the most important part first, so your team starts using it sooner.

Why not use ready-made software like Tally or Zoho?

If a ready-made tool fits your process, it is often the better choice and we will tell you so. Custom software makes sense when your process is unique, when you need several tools to work together, or when per-user fees become expensive.

Can it connect to the software we already use?

Usually yes. We can connect to Excel, Google Sheets, payment gateways, WhatsApp, email and most software that can export data or has an API.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes a free support period. After that, you can choose an optional monthly support plan for updates, backups and new features.
