AI chatbot for clinics: uses, cost and setup

By Agam Sahu, founder of Kodivo · Updated 28 October 2026 · 5 min read

Most clinics get the same questions every day: timings, fees, doctor availability and directions. An AI chatbot can answer these instantly on your website and WhatsApp, book appointments, and let your staff focus on patients at the desk.

What a clinic chatbot can do

  • Answer common questions 24×7: timings, doctors, fees, location, parking, insurance.
  • Book appointments: show free slots and confirm the booking.
  • Send reminders: appointment reminders and follow-up visit reminders on WhatsApp.
  • Share updates: tell patients when reports are ready.
  • Collect enquiries: save the name, phone number and need of every new patient.
  • Reply in Hindi and English, the way your patients write.

A sample conversation

Patient: Is Dr. Sharma available tomorrow evening?

Chatbot: Yes, Dr. Sharma is available tomorrow from 5 PM to 8 PM. Slots are free at 5:30 PM and 6:15 PM. Shall I book one for you?

Patient: 6:15 please.

Chatbot: Done. Your appointment is booked for tomorrow at 6:15 PM. You'll get a reminder on WhatsApp 2 hours before.

You can try a similar demo on our AI demo page.

What a chatbot should not do

A clinic chatbot is a receptionist, not a doctor. A good setup:

  • Never gives a diagnosis or medicine advice. It asks the patient to consult the doctor.
  • Sends emergencies to a person or tells the patient to call the clinic or emergency services.
  • Answers only from your clinic's information, so it doesn't make things up.
  • Hands the chat to your staff when it is not sure.

Patient data and privacy

Health information is sensitive. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you should take consent, collect only what you need, and keep it secure. Ask your developer where the data is stored, who can see it, and how it is deleted.

FAQ

Quick answers

Will the chatbot replace my receptionist?

No. It handles repeated questions and bookings, so your receptionist has more time for patients at the clinic.

Can patients book appointments on WhatsApp?

Yes. With the WhatsApp Business Platform, patients can see free slots, book and get reminders in the same chat.

What if the chatbot gives a wrong answer?

It answers only from your clinic's information and hands the chat to your staff when it is not sure. You can review conversations at any time.
