Earn money from home in India: an honest guide for tech and non-tech people

By Agam Sahu, founder of Kodivo · Updated 30 October 2026 · 9 min read

Lost a job, waiting for the next one, or just want extra income? Good. There are real ways to earn from home in India. But there are also many scams, and no honest method pays big money from day one. This guide shows real options, how long each takes, what it costs to start, and the warning signs of fraud.

The one rule to remember: real work pays you. If anyone asks you to pay a fee, a deposit or a "registration charge" to get work, it is almost always a scam.

For everyone (no technical skills needed)

1. Online tuition

Teach a school subject, spoken English, or a skill like music or drawing on Google Meet or Zoom. Start with friends, relatives and local parents' WhatsApp groups. Platforms like Superprof also list home and online tutors.

  • Need: a phone or laptop, good internet, a quiet corner.
  • Tip: offer one free demo class, then charge per month. Small batches of 3–5 students earn more per hour than one-to-one.

2. Home food business

Tiffin service, cakes, pickles, snacks or festival sweets for your area. Take orders on WhatsApp Business (free catalog and quick replies) and show your food on Instagram.

  • Need: an FSSAI registration. Food businesses must have one, and small home businesses can get the basic registration online.
  • Tip: a free Google Business Profile helps people nearby find you on Google Maps.

3. Selling products online

Sell clothes, home items, handmade products or local specialities on Meesho, Amazon or Flipkart, or handmade items to buyers abroad on Etsy.

  • Need: a bank account, PAN, and GST details. Small sellers can often start with a GST enrolment ID instead of full registration; check each platform's current rules.
  • Tip: start with 5–10 products you understand well. Good photos and honest descriptions reduce returns.

4. Writing, translation, voice-over and virtual assistant work

Businesses need blog posts, product descriptions, Hindi or regional translations, voice-overs for videos, and help with emails, scheduling and data. Find work on Fiverr, Upwork, LinkedIn and Internshala (filter for "work from home").

  • Need: 3–5 good samples. Make them yourself if you don't have clients yet.
  • Tip: AI tools can draft faster, but always check and improve the result. Clients pay for quality, not for copy-paste.

5. Content and affiliate income (slow but long-lasting)

Make videos or posts about something you know well: cooking, exam tips, product reviews, local guides. Income can come from ads, sponsorships and affiliate links (Amazon Associates and similar programs pay a small commission when someone buys through your link).

  • Need: patience. YouTube and Instagram have minimum requirements before they pay, and growing usually takes months.
  • Tip: pick one topic and one platform, and post regularly for 3 months before judging.

For tech people (developers, designers, testers, marketers)

6. Freelancing for small businesses

Clinics, coaching centres, shops and restaurants need websites, Google listings, WhatsApp automation and social media help. Most of them are within 10 km of you. Upwork, Fiverr and LinkedIn work for clients outside your city.

  • Tip: build 2–3 sample projects, show clear fixed prices, and deliver fast. See our website cost guide for typical Indian prices.

7. AI automation for businesses

Small businesses want chatbots for FAQs, WhatsApp auto-replies, lead tracking in Google Sheets, and reports made automatically. If you can connect APIs and tools, this is one of the fastest-growing kinds of work.

  • Tip: learn the basics in our free AI course, then build one demo bot for a type of business you know.

8. Mock interviews, mentoring and teaching

With 2+ years of experience, you can take paid mock interviews, resume reviews or doubt sessions. Platforms like Topmate let you list paid 1:1 sessions, or you can share a booking link on LinkedIn.

  • Tip: our free frontend and backend interview questions are a good base for your own sessions.

9. Digital products

Sell templates, notes, Notion or Excel trackers, UI kits or small tools once, many times. Use a payment page (Razorpay, Instamojo) or a platform like Gumroad.

10. Remote jobs and AI training work

Many companies hire remotely; filter by "Remote" on LinkedIn, Naukri, Wellfound and Indeed. AI companies also hire people to write, check and rate AI answers, including coding tasks. This work is real but often irregular, so treat it as extra income, and apply only through the company's official website.

⚠️ Scam alert: how to spot a fake work-from-home offer

Job losses bring more scams. Be careful if you see any of these:

  • They ask for a registration fee, security deposit, kit charge or "training fee" before giving work.
  • "Like YouTube videos", "rate hotels" or "review products" tasks on Telegram or WhatsApp that pay small amounts first, then ask you to "invest" to unlock bigger tasks.
  • A job offer from an unknown number with very high pay for very little work, and no proper interview.
  • They ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, bank login or to install a screen-sharing app.
  • "Rent your account" offers: someone pays you to use your bank account, UPI, SIM or email. Criminals use such accounts for fraud and even bomb-threat emails, and the account owner is held responsible. In October 2026, a man in Kanpur was arrested for selling email accounts that were later used for threats to schools. Learn more.
  • "Data entry" or "form filling" jobs that end with a fake legal notice demanding money.
  • Crypto or trading groups promising fixed daily returns.

If you are cheated: call the national cyber crime helpline 1930 immediately, or report at cybercrime.gov.in. Reporting fast improves the chance of stopping the money.

A simple 30-day plan

  1. Days 1–3: choose one option from the table above. Don't try five at once.
  2. Days 4–10: make 3 samples or a demo (a sample class, a menu with photos, 3 product listings, 2 demo projects).
  3. Days 11–20: tell 30 people: friends, past colleagues, local groups, LinkedIn. Offer a small first-customer discount.
  4. Days 21–30: deliver well, ask happy customers for a review or referral, and fix what didn't work.

Money basics

  • Keep a simple record of what you earn and spend from day one.
  • Income from freelancing and selling is taxable, so file your income tax return. Above certain yearly limits, GST registration is also needed. A CA can set this up quickly.
  • If you are employed, check your employment contract before taking side work.
FAQ

Quick answers

Can I really earn from home without any investment?

Yes, with skills you already have, like tuition, writing, translation or freelancing. You need a phone or laptop and internet. Selling products or a food business needs a small amount for stock and registration.

How much can I earn from home?

It depends on your skill, the time you give and how well you find customers. Most people earn a little in the first months and grow with reviews and referrals. Be careful of anyone promising a fixed high income.

Are data entry jobs from home real?

Some are, usually through real companies on LinkedIn, Naukri or Internshala. But data entry is also the most common scam. If you are asked to pay anything, or the offer comes from an unknown number on WhatsApp or Telegram, stay away.

Is it safe to rent my bank account, SIM or email account for money?

No. This is how criminals get "mule accounts" for fraud and threats. Because the account is in your name, you can be arrested along with them. Never sell, rent or share your bank, UPI, SIM or email account.

What should I do if I paid a work-from-home scammer?

Call the cyber crime helpline 1930 immediately and report at cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank. The faster you report, the better the chance of stopping the money.

I am a developer who lost my job. What is the fastest way to earn?

Freelance projects for small businesses, paid mock interviews and AI automation work usually start paying fastest. Keep applying for jobs at the same time, and use our free interview question pages to prepare.
