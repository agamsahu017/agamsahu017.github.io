FAQ

Pricing questions

Are these prices fixed?

Yes. After a free consultation you get a written quote with the exact scope, price and timeline. The price doesn't change unless you ask for extra work.

How do I pay?

In milestones: a small advance to start, and the rest after you see the work. Packages under ₹5,000 are usually 50% advance and 50% on delivery.

What is not included in the price?

Third-party charges are paid directly by you, at cost: domain (about ₹800–1,200 a year), paid hosting if needed, WhatsApp Business API message charges and AI usage charges. We explain them before you decide.

Do I have to take a monthly plan?

No. Monthly plans are optional. Every package includes a free support period after launch.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Start with Starter and move to Growth or AI Pro any time. You only pay the difference for the new work.
