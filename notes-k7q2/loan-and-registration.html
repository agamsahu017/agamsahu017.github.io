Kodivo: Registration and Loan Steps

Updated 13 Nov 2026. Rules and interest rates change often, so confirm the final details with the bank or the DIC.

The right order (follow these steps one by one)

  1. Check CIBIL score (Day 1, from home): cibil.com gives one free report every year. Check the score of both your wife and your brother (you need their PAN and mobile number). 700+ is good, 750+ is very good.
  2. Choose the owner (Day 1–2): your wife or your brother. Pick the person with a good CIBIL score and no unpaid loans. If your wife is the owner, PMEGP gives a bigger subsidy, but the loan and all responsibility will be in her name.
  3. Udyam registration (Day 2, free, 15 minutes): go to udyamregistration.gov.in → the owner's Aadhaar OTP + PAN → business name "Kodivo", type Proprietary, activity Services.
  4. Open a current account (Day 3–7): first call the bank and ask which documents they need. If the bank asks for one more proof, get a Shop & Establishment registration (UP Labour portal or a CSC centre). At the branch, give PAN, Aadhaar, photo, Udyam certificate and address proof. You get a current account and a UPI QR code in the name "Kodivo". It is easier to take the loan from this same bank.
  5. Visit the DIC (District Industries Centre, Lucknow) (Week 2): the owner must go in person. Take Aadhaar, PAN, Udyam certificate, bank passbook and the project report. Ask: how much PMEGP subsidy can we get, when is the EDP training, and which bank gives loans quickly.
  6. Apply for the loan (Week 2–3):
    • MUDRA Shishu (₹50,000): apply at your bank branch with the project report. Usually takes 2–4 weeks.
    • PMEGP: apply online at kviconline.gov.in → bank checks the application → EDP training → loan + subsidy. Takes 1–3 months.
In short: CIBIL → owner → Udyam → current account → DIC → loan

How much interest?

About 9–12% a year (government banks are often cheaper). For ₹50,000 over 3 years:

  • MUDRA Shishu: no security (collateral) needed, and usually no processing fee.
  • PMEGP: normal interest. The benefit is the subsidy, which the bank keeps in an FD for 3 years.
  • Ask the bank: the exact rate (EBLR/MCLR + spread), any processing fee, and any discount for paying EMIs on time. Compare 2 banks.

If the business makes a loss, is the loan forgiven?

No. The MUDRA guarantee (CGFMU) protects the bank, not you. The PMEGP subsidy is adjusted only if the business runs for 3 years.

  • In a proprietorship, the owner is personally responsible for the loan.
  • EMI not paid for 90 days → the loan becomes an NPA → the owner's CIBIL score drops, and the bank starts recovery.
  • If money is tight, go to the bank before you miss an EMI and ask to restructure the loan or pause EMIs (moratorium). A one-time settlement (OTS) is possible, but CIBIL will show "Settled", which looks bad.
  • Take the loan only if the ₹1,637 EMI can be paid even without business income. Spend it only on real needs (laptop, tools).
