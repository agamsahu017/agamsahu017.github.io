Kodivo: Registration aur Loan ke Steps

Updated 13 Nov 2026. Rules aur rates badalte rehte hain, final baat bank / DIC se confirm karein.

Sahi order (isi sequence mein)

  1. CIBIL check (Day 1, ghar se): cibil.com pe saal mein ek free report milti hai. Wife aur bhai dono ka score dekho (PAN + mobile chahiye). 700+ achha, 750+ bahut achha.
  2. Owner tay karo (Day 1–2): wife ya bhai. Jiska CIBIL achha ho aur koi loan pending na ho. Wife owner ho toh PMEGP mein zyada subsidy, lekin loan aur zimmedari unke naam pe.
  3. Udyam registration (Day 2, free, 15 min): udyamregistration.gov.in → owner ka Aadhaar OTP + PAN → naam "Kodivo", Proprietary, Services.
  4. Current account (Day 3–7): pehle bank ko call karke documents poochho. Bank aur proof maange toh Shop & Establishment (UP Labour portal ya CSC). Branch mein PAN, Aadhaar, photo, Udyam certificate, address proof → current account + "Kodivo" UPI QR. Loan isi bank se lena aasaan rahega.
  5. DIC (Zila Udyog Kendra, Lucknow) (Week 2): owner khud jaaye. Saath mein: Aadhaar, PAN, Udyam certificate, passbook, project report. Poochho: PMEGP subsidy kitni milegi, EDP training kab hai, kaunsa bank jaldi loan deta hai.
  6. Loan apply (Week 2–3):
    • MUDRA Shishu (₹50k): seedha bank branch mein, project report ke saath. Aam taur pe 2–4 hafte.
    • PMEGP: kviconline.gov.in pe online → bank verification → EDP training → loan + subsidy. 1–3 mahine.
Short mein: CIBIL → owner → Udyam → current account → DIC → loan

  • MUDRA Shishu: koi security nahi, processing fee aksar nahi.
  • PMEGP: byaaj normal hai, fayda subsidy ka hai (3 saal FD mein rehti hai).
  • Bank se poochho: exact rate (EBLR/MCLR + spread), processing fee, time pe EMI pe chhoot. 2 banks compare karo.

Nuksaan hua toh loan maaf hoga?

Nahi. MUDRA ki guarantee (CGFMU) bank ke liye hai, aapke liye nahi. PMEGP subsidy business 3 saal chalne pe hi adjust hoti hai.

  • Proprietorship mein owner personally zimmedar hai.
  • 90 din EMI nahi bhari → NPA → owner ka CIBIL kharab, recovery.
  • Mushkil ho toh EMI rukne se pehle bank se restructuring / moratorium maango. OTS ka option hai par CIBIL pe "Settled" aata hai.
  • ₹1,637 ki EMI bina business ke bhi bhari ja sake, tabhi loan lo. Paisa sirf asli zaroorat (laptop, tools) pe.
