CodeAwadh is a small, founder-led tech studio. Agam Sahu personally leads every project, so you get senior-level work and one person who is accountable from start to finish. For larger projects, trusted specialists are added as needed.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes! Shops, clinics, coaching centres, hotels, startups — whatever your size, we'll suggest a solution that fits your budget.

Can we meet in person?

Yes. I work with Delhi NCR clients (Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Ghaziabad) and Lucknow clients regularly. We start on call/video and meet in person for key discussions when needed. Most projects run smoothly fully online.

Are classes online or offline?

Weekend batches and mentoring are live online, so you can join from anywhere. Recordings and notes are shared.

I'm a beginner. Can I join?

If you know basic programming, yes. Message me on WhatsApp and I'll tell you honestly if the batch is right for you.
