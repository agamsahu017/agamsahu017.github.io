How to get your business on Google Maps

By Agam Sahu, founder of Kodivo · Updated 28 October 2026 · 5 min read

When someone searches "clinic near me" or "cafe in Noida", Google shows a map with nearby businesses. To appear there, you need a free Google Business Profile. Here is how to set it up and get more customers from it.

What is a Google Business Profile?

It is your free business listing on Google Search and Google Maps. It shows your name, address, phone number, timings, photos, reviews and a link to your website. For local businesses, it is often the first thing a new customer sees.

How to set it up: step by step

  1. Go to google.com/business and sign in with a Google account you will keep long term.
  2. Enter your business name exactly as it appears on your board or bills.
  3. Choose the main category that best describes you, like "Dental clinic" or "Coaching center".
  4. Add your address and place the pin correctly on the map. If you visit customers instead, choose a service area.
  5. Add your phone number and website.
  6. Verify your business. Google will tell you the method, for example a short video of your shop or office, a phone call or SMS, or email.
  7. After verification, complete everything: timings, services, photos and a short description.

7 tips to appear higher on Google Maps

  • Pick the right categories. One main category, plus a few extra ones that truly match your services.
  • Add real photos. Your shop front, inside, team and work. Add new ones every month.
  • Keep timings correct, including holidays and festival days.
  • Ask happy customers for reviews, and reply politely to every review, good or bad.
  • Use the same name, address and phone everywhere: your website, Justdial, Facebook and Instagram.
  • Post updates such as offers, new services or events.
  • Link a good website. It gives Google and customers more information about you.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Adding keywords to your business name, like "Sharma Dental Best Dentist Lucknow". This is against Google's rules and can get your profile suspended.
  • Buying fake reviews. Google removes them, and it can harm your listing.
  • Creating duplicate profiles for the same location.
  • Using an employee's personal email. If they leave, you may lose access. Use a business account you control.

How to get more reviews

  1. Copy your review link from your Google Business Profile ("Ask for reviews").
  2. Send it on WhatsApp after a good experience, with a short thank-you message.
  3. Put a QR code with the link at your counter or reception.
  4. Reply to every review within a few days.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Creating and managing a Google Business Profile is free. Be careful of callers who ask for money to "activate" or "verify" it on Google's behalf.

How long does it take to show on Google Maps?

Usually a few days after verification, though it can take longer. Ranking higher takes time and depends on reviews, distance and how complete your profile is.

Can I list a business that works from home?

Yes. If customers don't visit you, set a service area and hide your home address.
