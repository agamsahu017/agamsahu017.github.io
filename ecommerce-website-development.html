Honest advice

Custom store or Shopify?

  • A ready platform like Shopify is quick to start, but has monthly fees, and some features need paid apps.
  • A custom store costs more at the start, but has no monthly platform fee, fits your exact process, and the code is yours.
  • If a ready platform suits your business better, we will tell you honestly in the free call.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does an e-commerce website cost?

Our custom online stores start at ₹60,000. The final price depends on the number of products, payment and delivery options, and extra features like AI search. You get a fixed quote before work starts.

How long does it take to build an online store?

Usually 3 to 6 weeks, depending on features and how quickly product photos and details are ready.

What are the running costs?

A domain (about ₹800–1,200 a year), hosting, and the payment gateway's fee on each online payment, which is usually around 2% in India. We explain all costs before you decide.

Can I add and edit products myself?

Yes. The admin panel is simple, and we train you and your staff to add products, change prices and manage orders.

Can you move my store from another platform?

Yes. We can move your products, customers and orders from your current platform in most cases.
