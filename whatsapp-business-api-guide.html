WhatsApp Business API explained simply

By Agam Sahu, founder of Kodivo · Updated 28 October 2026 · 6 min read

If your business gets many WhatsApp messages, you have probably heard of the "WhatsApp Business API". Here is what it is, how it differs from the free WhatsApp Business app, how the charges work, and when it is worth it.

Business app vs Business API

WhatsApp Business appWhatsApp Business API (Platform)
CostFreePay per message for some message types
Used onYour phone (plus linked devices)Connected to software, chatbots and your website
AutomationBasic: greeting, away message, quick repliesFull: AI chatbots, bookings, reminders, order updates
Team useA few devicesMany staff on one number, with a shared inbox
Best forSmall shops and single ownersGrowing businesses with many chats

Simple rule: if you or one person can reply to all chats on the phone, the free app is enough. If chats are being missed, or you want automatic replies, bookings and reminders, the API makes sense.

How do you get the API?

Meta (the company behind WhatsApp) provides the platform. You can connect to it directly through Meta's Cloud API, or through an official partner (called a Business Solution Provider) that gives you a ready dashboard. A developer then connects it to your chatbot, website or software.

You will usually need:

  • A Meta Business account, ideally verified with your business documents
  • A phone number for WhatsApp. Ask your developer whether your current number can stay on the normal app at the same time, as this depends on the setup
  • A display name that matches your business

How the charges work

Meta charges for template messages: pre-approved messages that your business starts, like reminders or offers. There are three main types:

  • Marketing: offers, new arrivals, festival greetings. These cost the most.
  • Utility: order updates, appointment reminders, payment receipts. These cost less.
  • Authentication: one-time passwords (OTPs).

When a customer messages you first, you can reply freely within 24 hours. Replies inside this window are generally not charged. Rates change from time to time, so always check Meta's current pricing for India before you plan a campaign. Partners may add their own monthly fee on top.

The 24-hour rule

After a customer's last message, you have a 24-hour window to reply with any normal message. After 24 hours, you can only start a conversation with an approved template message. This rule stops businesses from spamming people.

Opt-in: ask before you message

You must have the customer's permission (opt-in) before sending them messages, for example a checkbox on your website form. If people block or report your messages, WhatsApp can limit your number. Send useful messages, not spam.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is the WhatsApp Business API free?

No. Getting access to the platform is free, but Meta charges for template messages your business starts. Replies within 24 hours of a customer's message are generally not charged. Partners may also charge a monthly fee.

Do I get a green tick?

Not automatically. Verified badges depend on Meta's rules and verification at the time you apply. A verified business account and a good messaging record help.

Can I send bulk messages?

Only to customers who have agreed to receive them, and only with approved template messages. Sending unwanted messages can get your number limited.
