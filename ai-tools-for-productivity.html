AI tools that replace old ways of working: for developers and non-developers

By Agam Sahu, founder of Kodivo · Updated 30 October 2026 · 8 min read

Most of us still work the old way: searching 10 links, typing notes by hand, fighting with Excel formulas. AI tools can now do much of this in minutes. Here is a simple list: what you do today, what AI can do instead, and which tool to try first.

How to read this page: "Free" means a useful free plan exists. "Paid" means you need a subscription for the main feature. Prices and features change often, so check the tool's website before paying. We review this list every 3 months.

Developer rule: AI writes the first draft; you are still responsible for it. Read every change, run the tests, and never let AI handle secrets or production data without checks. Learn the basics in our free AI course and see how AI APIs work in calling an LLM API.

5 safety rules for using AI at work

  1. Don't paste private data: customer details, Aadhaar or PAN numbers, passwords, salary data or company code, unless your company allows that tool.
  2. Follow your company's AI policy. Many companies allow only approved tools at work.
  3. Check facts, numbers and laws. AI can sound confident and still be wrong.
  4. Keep your own skills sharp. Use AI to go faster at things you understand, and to learn new things, not to avoid understanding.
  5. Be careful with "AI" apps that ask for payment upfront or promise money. Many scams now use the word AI. See our guide to earning from home safely.

Start in 7 days

  1. Day 1: use voice typing for all your messages.
  2. Day 2: ask a chatbot (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini) to explain one thing you never understood at work.
  3. Day 3: use Perplexity or AI search instead of Google for a real question.
  4. Day 4: upload a long PDF to NotebookLM and ask it 5 questions.
  5. Day 5: make one poster or slide deck with Canva or Gamma.
  6. Day 6: ask AI for one Excel or Sheets formula you need (developers: let an AI assistant write tests for one function).
  7. Day 7: note which tool saved you the most time, and make it a daily habit.
FAQ

Quick answers

Which AI tool should I learn first?

Start with one general chatbot (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini). It helps with writing, explaining, translating and planning. Then add one tool for your main work: NotebookLM for studying, Canva for design, or GitHub Copilot or Cursor for coding.

Are these AI tools free?

Most have useful free plans. Paid plans add more usage, better models or built-in features in Office and Google Workspace. Try the free plan for a few weeks before paying.

Will AI take my job?

AI is changing how work is done. People who use AI well will often do more work in less time than people who don't. Learning these tools is one of the best ways to stay valuable.

Is it safe to upload office files to AI tools?

Only if your company allows that tool. Never upload customer data, passwords, financial data or private code to a personal AI account.

How often is this list updated?

We review it every 3 months, because AI tools and prices change quickly. The date at the top shows the last update.
