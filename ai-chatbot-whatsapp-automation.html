How much does an AI chatbot cost?

Setup starts at ₹4,499 with our launch offer for the first 10 clients. There are small running costs for AI usage and, on WhatsApp, Meta's own message charges. We explain these clearly before you decide.

Will the chatbot give wrong answers?

We set it up to answer only from your information. When it isn't sure, it says so and hands the chat to you. Important details like fees can also come straight from your records.

Does it work on WhatsApp?

Yes. Full WhatsApp automation uses Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. We help you set it up on your business number.

Can it reply in Hindi?

Yes. It understands and replies in Hindi, English and Hinglish.

Can I see the conversations?

Yes. You can see conversations and every lead the assistant captures.
