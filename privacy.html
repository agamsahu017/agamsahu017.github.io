Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Kodivo ("we", "us") is a software studio founded by Agam Sahu, based in Delhi NCR and Lucknow, India. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email and project details when you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Website forms: when you fill the consultation or free session form, we save your name, mobile number, city and the details you choose to share in our database, only to contact you about your request. Ask us any time and we'll delete it.
  • Website: this website does not use cookies. Your theme (light/dark) and language choices are saved only in your own browser.
  • Visitor statistics: to count daily visitors, we record the page you open, your country, and whether you use a mobile or desktop. Your IP address is turned into a one-way code that changes every day, and the IP itself is never stored, so we can't identify you or follow you across days. These statistics are private and are never shared or sold.
  • AI chatbot demo: the demo runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is stored or sent to us.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project or classes.
  • To prepare proposals, agreements and invoices.
  • We never sell or rent your information to anyone.

Client projects and data

When we build software for you, your business data and your customers' data belong to you. We access it only to build, test and support your project, and we follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Specific terms are written into each project agreement.

Third-party services

We use WhatsApp, email and phone to communicate. Their own privacy policies apply to messages sent through them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time.
