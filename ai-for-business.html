FAQ

Questions business owners ask

We are not a tech company. Can we still use AI?

Yes. You don't need a technical team. We build it, train your staff, and keep it running. Your team only uses a simple screen or WhatsApp.

Can it work with the software we already use?

Usually yes. We can connect to Excel, Google Sheets, email, WhatsApp, your website and most software that can export data or has an API. We check this in the free call.

What if the AI gives a wrong answer?

We set the AI to answer only from your own information, show where the answer came from, and pass the question to a person when it is not sure. Important actions, like payments or deleting data, always need a human to approve.

Is our data safe?

Yes. We sign an NDA, give access only to the people who need it, and follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Your data is not used to train public AI models.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

Most business AI projects cost between ₹75,000 and ₹10,00,000 and take 3 to 12 weeks, depending on the size. We often start with a small pilot so you can see results before a bigger investment.

Are there running costs after launch?

Yes, small ones. AI services and hosting charge by usage, and these are paid by you directly, at cost. We estimate them before you decide, and offer optional support plans.
