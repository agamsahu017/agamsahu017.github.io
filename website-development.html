FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a business website cost?

Websites start at ₹2,999 with our launch offer for the first 10 clients. The price depends on the number of pages and features like booking, payments or an admin panel. You always get a fixed quote before work starts.

How long does it take?

Usually 5–7 days for a standard business website, once we have your text, photos and prices.

What are the yearly costs after launch?

A domain usually costs about ₹800–1,200 a year. Simple websites can often be hosted for free or at low cost, and we explain the options before you decide.

Will my website rank on Google?

We set up on-page SEO properly so Google can find and understand your site. Ranking for competitive searches takes time and also depends on reviews and your Google Business Profile, which we can help you set up.

Do you build online stores too?

Yes. See e-commerce website development. For a full price breakdown, read our guide: how much does a website cost in India?

Can I add an AI chatbot later?

Yes. Every website we build is ready for an AI chatbot and WhatsApp automation, which can be added any time.
