Website or Instagram page: which does your business need?

By Agam Sahu, founder of Kodivo · Updated 28 October 2026 · 4 min read

Many business owners ask: "I already have an Instagram page. Do I really need a website?" Both are useful, but they do different jobs. Here is a simple comparison to help you decide.

The quick comparison

Instagram pageBusiness website
CostFreeOne-time cost, plus about ₹800–1,200 a year for the domain
Found on GoogleRarelyYes, when people search for your service and city
Who owns itInstagram's rules apply. Accounts can be hacked or blockedYou own the domain, content and data
Bookings & paymentsThrough DMs, done by handBooking forms, online payments, WhatsApp buttons
InformationScattered across postsServices, prices, timings and FAQs in one place
Best atShowing photos and reels, reaching new peopleBuilding trust and turning visitors into customers

When Instagram alone is enough

  • You are just starting and testing your idea
  • Your customers mostly find you through reels and friends
  • You sell a few products and handle every order personally on DM

When you need a website

  • People search on Google for your service, like "dentist near me" or "coaching in Gomti Nagar"
  • You want customers to book, pay or send enquiries without waiting for a reply
  • You deal with bigger clients, companies or parents who check you out before deciding
  • You run ads and need a page that clearly explains your offer
  • You don't want your business to depend on one app's rules

Our advice: use both, for different jobs

Think of Instagram as your shop window and your website as your shop counter. Instagram helps people discover you. Your website gives them the details, builds trust, and makes it easy to book or buy. Put your website link in your Instagram bio, and your Instagram link on your website.

FAQ

Quick answers

Can I skip the website and use only Instagram?

Yes, when you are just starting. As you grow, a website helps you appear on Google, look more trustworthy and take bookings without manual replies.

Will a website help my Instagram?

Yes. A website link in your bio gives followers one place for prices, timings and booking, which turns more followers into customers.

Is a Google Business Profile the same as a website?

No. A Google Business Profile shows your business on Google Maps and search. It is free and very useful, and works best together with a website.
