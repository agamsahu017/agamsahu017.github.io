2. Paying in instalments

  • 1-year and 2-year courses can be paid in 3 instalments: 40% at admission, 30% within 60 days, and 30% within 120 days.
  • Foundation and Board Booster fees must be paid in full at admission.
  • If an instalment is late by more than 15 days, a late fee of Rs 1,000 is charged, and access to the test series is paused until it is paid.
  • Payment by UPI, card, net banking or cheque. Cash up to Rs 20,000 only.

3. Scholarships

  • BrightPath Scholarship Test (BST): held on the first Sunday of every month from January to June. Fee Rs 200. Top scorers get 10% to 90% off the tuition fee.
  • Board marks: students with 90% or more in Class X boards get 15% off. 95% or more gets 25% off.
  • Girl students get an extra 5% off on JEE courses.
  • Only the highest one scholarship is given, plus the girl-student 5% if it applies. Scholarship does not apply to the registration fee.

4. Refund policy

  • The registration fee is never refunded.
  • If a student leaves within 15 days of the batch start date, 90% of the fee paid (excluding registration fee) is refunded.
  • If a student leaves between 16 and 30 days after the batch starts, 50% of the fee paid is refunded.
  • After 30 days, no refund is given.
  • To ask for a refund, fill the refund form at the front desk with the original fee receipt. The refund is sent to the bank account within 21 working days.
  • A student may change from one batch to another of the same course once, free of cost, within the first 30 days.

6. Tests and results

  • A weekly test is held every Sunday from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM at the centre. Results come in the app by Tuesday evening.
  • A full syllabus mock test (in the real exam pattern) is held on the last Sunday of every month.
  • Parents get the test result and attendance by SMS every month.
  • A student who misses a weekly test can take it online in the app within 3 days, but the marks are not counted in the rank list.

7. Attendance

  • Attendance is marked with the student ID card at the gate.
  • Students must keep at least 80% attendance. Below 80%, parents are called for a meeting.
  • If a student is absent for 7 days in a row without informing, the seat may be given to a student on the waiting list. Fees are not refunded in this case.
  • Recorded lectures of every class are available in the app for 7 days, for students who miss a class.

8. Doubt sessions

  • Doubt counters are open Monday to Saturday, 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM, for all subjects.
  • Doubts can also be posted in the app. A teacher replies within 24 hours (except Sunday).
  • One-to-one sessions with a faculty member can be booked once a week, for 20 minutes, from the app.

9. Rules of conduct

  • Mobile phones must be switched off and kept in the bag during class. A phone used in class is kept until the end of the day.
  • Students must wear the BrightPath ID card at all times inside the centre. A lost ID card is replaced for Rs 150.
  • Eating is allowed only in the cafeteria on the ground floor.
  • Sharing study material or test papers outside the institute, or recording classes, can lead to cancellation of admission without refund.

10. Facilities

  • Air-conditioned classrooms, a self-study library open 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM (also on Sunday), and RO drinking water on every floor.
  • Hostel and PG help: the front desk can share a list of nearby hostels. BrightPath does not run its own hostel.
  • Parking for bicycles and two-wheelers in the basement. No car parking.
