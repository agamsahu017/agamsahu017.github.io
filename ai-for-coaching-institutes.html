How AI can help coaching institutes

By Agam Sahu, founder of Kodivo · Updated 28 October 2026 · 5 min read

Coaching institutes handle hundreds of enquiries, fee follow-ups and parent calls every month, often with a small team. AI won't replace your teachers, but it can take over a lot of the repeated work around them.

1. Answer admission enquiries 24×7

Parents and students message late at night asking about batches, fees, timings and demo classes. An AI assistant on your website and WhatsApp can answer instantly from your own information, collect the student's details and book a demo class. Your counsellor then calls only the serious leads.

2. Follow up every lead automatically

Many admissions are lost because nobody followed up. Every enquiry can be saved in one place, with automatic WhatsApp reminders like "Your free demo class is tomorrow at 5 PM" and a follow-up after the demo.

3. Fee reminders and receipts

Polite fee reminders go out automatically before the due date, and receipts are sent on WhatsApp after payment. No more awkward calls or registers.

4. Attendance and progress updates for parents

Parents get a message when a student is absent, and a simple monthly report with attendance and test scores. This builds trust and reduces dropouts.

5. Prepare tests and practice questions faster

AI can draft practice questions, answer keys and simple explanations from your syllabus and notes. Teachers must always review them, because AI can make mistakes. Used this way, it saves hours of typing every week.

6. A doubt assistant based on your notes

Students can ask questions and get answers based on your own study material, any time. It should clearly tell students to ask a teacher when it is not sure, and it works best for revision, not as a replacement for classes.

Things to be careful about

  • Children's data: most students are under 18. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 requires parents' consent to process children's personal data. Collect only what you need and keep it secure.
  • Accuracy: AI-generated questions and answers must be checked by a teacher.
  • Spam: send only useful messages to parents and students who have agreed to receive them.
