Find a developer or QA job: North India and remote

A practical guide for job seekers: where the tech jobs are in North India, how to find genuine remote jobs open to India, what to prepare at each experience level, and how to avoid fake job offers.

The honest truth for 2026: the market is competitive and layoffs have added many experienced candidates. What works best: a focused profile for one role, referrals, applying early to fresh postings, and strong interview preparation. Mass-applying with one generic resume rarely works.

Where to search for North India jobs

  • LinkedIn Jobs: filter by city and "On-site" or "Hybrid". Also post that you're open to work and message recruiters directly.
  • Naukri and Foundit: the biggest Indian portals; keep your profile updated weekly, because recruiters search by recently updated profiles.
  • Instahyre, Cutshort and Hirist: tech-focused platforms with many product companies and startups.
  • Wellfound: startup jobs, often with the founders hiring directly.
  • Internshala: internships and fresher roles.
  • Company careers pages: make a list of 30 target companies in your city and check their careers pages weekly.
  • Referrals: the single best channel. Ask past colleagues, college seniors and LinkedIn connections.
  • Local tech meetups (Google Developer Groups, JavaScript and testing meetups): many jobs are filled through people you meet there.
  • Walk-in and weekend hiring drives: common at large service companies. Confirm the drive on the company's official website or careers page before going.

Remote jobs across the world: the real picture

  • Many "remote" jobs only accept people in the USA, Europe or one country. Look for Worldwide, Anywhere, APAC or India. Our live remote jobs page marks these for you.
  • Competition is global, so remote roles usually expect strong English, clear async writing, and 2+ years of experience. Freshers have better chances with Indian remote-first startups and internships.
  • Expect some timezone overlap, for example evening calls with US or European teams.

Being paid from abroad

  • You may be hired as an employee through an "employer of record" (EOR) service, or as an independent contractor who sends invoices.
  • Contractors receive money through their bank, or services like Wise or Payoneer. Keep the bank's inward remittance certificate (FIRC) for each payment.
  • Foreign income is taxable in India. For GST on export of services, ask a CA about filing a Letter of Undertaking (LUT).
  • If you are employed, check your contract before taking any remote side work.

Manual-only QA roles are shrinking. Moving to automation and API testing is the single best step for QA careers. The ISTQB Foundation certificate can help freshers get shortlisted, but a working automation project matters more.

Your application kit

  • Resume: one page up to about 5 years, two pages after. One target role. Results with numbers ("cut API time from 8 s to 120 ms").
  • Keywords: copy the exact skill words from the job post into your resume, if you really have them. Many companies filter resumes automatically.
  • GitHub or portfolio: pinned projects with a clear README and screenshots.
  • LinkedIn headline: role + stack + years, for example "Backend Developer · Node.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL · 4 yrs".

⚠️ Fake job offers: how to stay safe

  • Real employers never ask you to pay for registration, training, a laptop, "security deposit" or an offer letter.
  • Be careful with offers from personal email addresses (gmail.com etc.), interviews held only by chat on Telegram or WhatsApp, and offers with no interview at all.
  • Check the company: official website, LinkedIn page, employees on LinkedIn, and the job on the company's own careers page.
  • Never share OTPs, bank logins or Aadhaar copies before a verified offer, and never let anyone use your bank account, UPI or SIM (that is how mule-account crimes work).
  • Cheated? Call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in immediately.

Quick answers

Should I take a service company job or a startup job?

Service companies give stability, training and a known brand; startups give faster learning and ownership. For freshers either is fine; what matters most in the first 2 years is learning a lot and shipping real work.

Can a fresher get a remote job?

It's harder but possible, mostly through Indian remote-first startups, remote internships and open-source contributions. Many freshers start on-site or hybrid, then move to remote after 1–2 years of experience.

How many jobs should I apply to each week?

Quality beats volume: 15–25 targeted applications a week, each with a resume tuned to the post, plus 3–5 referral requests, usually works better than 200 generic applications.

Do I need certifications?

For developers, projects and interview skill matter more. For QA, ISTQB Foundation can help freshers; for cloud roles, an AWS or Azure certificate helps. None of them replaces real work you can show.
